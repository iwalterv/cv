# Párrafo de prueba de voz (mismo texto para las 3 candidatas)

~600 caracteres. Mezcla narración, ironía y un dato concreto, que es el tono del canal.

```
Congratulations. You've just been born in England, the year is 1347, and honestly? Your timing is terrible.
You're a peasant, which means you own nothing, you answer to a lord you've never met, and you'll spend
roughly two hundred days a year working his fields before you're allowed to touch your own.
Breakfast is bread. Lunch is bread. Dinner, if things are going well, is bread with a little pottage.
But don't worry about the long term. In about a year, a ship is going to dock in a small port called
Melcombe... and it's carrying something that will change your life. Briefly.
```

Notas del dato: la Peste Negra llegó a Inglaterra por Melcombe (Weymouth, Dorset) en junio de 1348. Fuente a citar en el guion real: Ziegler, *The Black Death* (1969), cap. 7; y Horrox, *The Black Death* (Manchester Univ. Press, 1994).
